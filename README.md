About The Project:
This project is an interactive Microsoft Excel dashboard designed to analyze Food Mart's sales performance and customer demographics across the USA, Mexico, and Canada. The goal is to transform raw retail data into clear, actionable business insights to help decision-makers understand revenue drivers, regional performance, and customer behavior.

Key Financial Highlights:
Total Revenue: $1,764,546
Total Cost: $711,728
Net Profit: $1,052,819 (Achieving a strong 59.7% profit margin)
Total Product Returns: 7,087 items

Main Insights & Findings:
Top Performing Region: The North West region is the main revenue driver, contributing around 48% of total profits ($505,783), with the USA leading in overall store transactions.
Product Demand: Product sales show healthy diversification across top items such as Monarch Rice Medly and Dollar Monthly Sports Magazine.
Customer Demographics: Customer gender distribution is balanced (50.4% Female vs 49.6% Male). The majority of customers are Bronze cardholders (5,703 members), showing a major opportunity to upgrade customers to higher loyalty tiers.

Excel Skills Used:
Data cleaning and preprocessing.
Pivot Tables and Pivot Charts for aggregation.
Advanced functions (SUMIFS, COUNTIFS, INDEX/MATCH).
Interactive Dashboard design using Slicers, KPI Cards, custom charts, and formatted tables.
Strategic Recommendations:
Double down on marketing and stock allocation in the North West region.
Create promotional campaigns to upgrade Bronze cardholders to Silver and Golden tiers.
Investigate the root causes behind the 7,087 returned products to minimize loss.
